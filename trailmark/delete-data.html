Delete your Trailmark data

Applies to Trailmark (dev.toufik.trailmark). Last updated: 21 October 2026.

Trailmark has no account and no server. All tracks, routes, segments, photos, videos and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app's storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Delete all data on your phone

  1. Open Settings on your Android phone.
  2. Tap Apps, then Trailmark.
  3. Tap Storage & cache.
  4. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data). This deletes all tracks, routes, segments, imported files, photos, videos and settings.

Alternatively, uninstall Trailmark. Uninstalling removes the app and all of its local data.

What is deleted

Recorded and imported tracks, routes, segments, statistics, offline map areas you downloaded, exported files stored in the app's folders, attached photos and videos, and all app settings.

What is kept

Nothing is kept by us, because we do not operate a server and do not receive your data. Purchases (subscriptions or the lifetime unlock) are processed by Google Play and are tied to your Google account. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Google Play subscriptions. Google Play retains purchase records under its own policies.

Retention period

No retention period applies on our side. Data exists only on your device until you clear storage or uninstall the app.
