Privacy Policy — AI Photo Studio Chat

Application ID: com.productchat.aiphotostudio · Android

Effective date: 29 September 2026

Photos never uploaded · No account · No ads

AI Photo Studio Chat edits product photos for online sellers. This policy explains exactly what happens to your images and your information.

1. The short version

Your photos are processed on your phone. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party, and the app has no account, no server, and no advertising. There is no analytics software.

2. Your photos never leave your device

This is the most important part of this policy.

When you choose a photo, the app reads it directly from your device's storage. Background removal, enhancement, shadow addition, relighting, and every other operation run in image-processing code executed on your phone. The result is written back to your device.

At no point is a photo transmitted to a remote server. There is no photo-processing API, no cloud inference endpoint, and no per-image upload fee. The app works fully offline for image processing.

You can remove every trace of your data at any time using the "Delete all data" control in Settings, which clears the app's history, saved images, settings, and any downloaded model files.

3. What the app stores on your device

  • Edited images you have chosen to save
  • Your editing history and timeline
  • Your credit balance and subscription status
  • Your saved brand presets (Pro)
  • Any promo or referral codes you have entered
  • App settings and the last-used options

All of this is stored in private application storage on your device.

4. The assistant and the image analysis

AI Photo Studio Chat lets you describe an edit in plain words instead of navigating menus. The instructions you type are interpreted on your device and applied using local image processing.

The app also includes a Smart Analysis feature that inspects a photo you select and suggests improvements based on its brightness, contrast, resolution, and background complexity. This inspection happens locally and is not transmitted anywhere.

5. Voice input

If you choose to use voice commands, your device's speech recognition service converts your speech into text. The resulting text is used to issue the edit instruction and is stored only in the conversation history on your device. The microphone is never recorded to a file or uploaded by this app.

7. What the app does NOT collect

The app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account details — there is no sign-up
  • Your photos or videos — they are processed locally and never uploaded
  • Your location or GPS coordinates
  • Your contacts, call log, or SMS messages
  • Your microphone recordings — voice input is converted to text by the device and not recorded by this app
  • Your installed apps or device identifiers such as IMEI or Android ID
  • Usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or profiling data

If the app encounters a technical error, the report is saved locally on your device only. It is never sent automatically.

9. In-app purchases

AI Photo Studio Chat offers a free tier with a limited number of edits per month, credit packs, monthly and yearly Pro plans, and a one-time lifetime purchase. All payments are processed by Google Play. The app receives confirmation that a purchase succeeded and stores a secure fingerprint of the purchase token to prevent duplicate credit grants. It does not receive or store card details.

10. Children

AI Photo Studio Chat is intended for adults running an online shop or creating product content. It is not directed at children under 13. Because it has no account and collects no personal information, it does not knowingly gather data from children.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR, CCPA and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data. All data is stored only on your device and never transmitted to us, so you can exercise every one of these rights yourself, including full deletion, from within the app.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date and noted in the app's release notes.

13. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support address listed on the app's Google Play store page.
