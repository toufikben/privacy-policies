Privacy Policy — Pulse (Private Journal)

Application ID: com.nabd.journal · Android

Effective date: 6 October 2026

Works offline · No account · No ads · No tracking

Pulse is a private journal that stores everything on your own device. This policy explains exactly what happens to your information when you use the app.

1. The short version

Your journal entries, moods, tags, photos and audio recordings never leave your phone. There is no account to create, no email address to give, and no server that receives your writing. The app contains no analytics software, no crash-reporting service, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

2. What stays on your device

Everything you create is written to your device's private storage:

  • Journal entries (text, titles, tags, pinned and favourite flags)
  • Mood records and your mood history
  • Photos you attach to entries
  • Audio recordings you make for an entry
  • Your seed garden, achievements, streaks, challenges and small habits
  • Letters to your future self
  • Tags, search preferences and app settings

All of this is stored encrypted: the journal database with AES-256, and photos and audio with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is generated on your device and held in the Android Keystore. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Pulse's data.

3. What leaves your device, and why

No journal content, mood data, photo, audio recording, name or e-mail address is ever included in these requests. Pulse contains no advertising, and no advertising identifier is used.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. Payment card details are never seen by this app and never stored on your device. A lifetime purchase is confirmed by Google Play alone.

Subscription checks (RevenueCat)

A subscription can renew, lapse or be refunded, so Pulse asks RevenueCat whether it is still active. Pulse sends the purchase token issued by Google Play, the product identifier and a random ID created on your phone, which identifies no person. RevenueCat checks the token with Google and returns the subscription's status and expiry date. This happens only for monthly or yearly subscriptions.

Dictation

Dictation uses your phone's on-device speech recognition by default, so your voice stays on the phone. If the offline voice pack for your language is missing, Pulse asks before using the phone's online speech service, which may send audio to its provider. You can withdraw that consent in Settings. Pulse itself keeps no audio from dictation.

4. What we do NOT collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any contact details
  • Any account or login information — the app has no sign-in
  • Your location or GPS coordinates
  • Your contacts, call log, or SMS messages
  • Your browsing history or installed apps
  • Usage analytics, advertising identifiers or profiling data
  • Your journal text, mood records, photos, or audio

5. On-device text analysis

Pulse offers features that look at your writing and moods — for example mood patterns, monthly summaries, habit insights and the smart reminder time. This runs entirely on your device using built-in rules. It is not artificial intelligence hosted in the cloud, and your text is not transmitted to any external service to be analysed.

Every permission is optional. If you decline one, the corresponding feature is unavailable and the rest of the app continues to work.

7. Your control over your data

  • Delete everything. Settings offers a control that erases all journal content, mood history, photos, audio and settings from your device in a single action.
  • Encrypted backup. You can export an encrypted, password-protected backup file and re-import it later. You choose where that file is stored; we never receive it.
  • App lock. You can require a fingerprint, face, or passcode every time the app opens.

8. Children

Pulse is not directed at children under 13. Because the app requires no account and collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly gather data from children.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, CCPA and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Because your journal is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us, you can exercise every one of these rights directly from within the app without contacting us. Subscription records held by RevenueCat are tied only to the random ID described in section 3.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date and noted in the app's release notes.

11. Contact
