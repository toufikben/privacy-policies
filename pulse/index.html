Privacy Policy — Pulse (Private Journal)

Application ID: com.nabd.journal · Android

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Works offline · No account · No ads · No tracking

Pulse is a private journal that stores everything on your own device. This policy explains exactly what happens to your information when you use the app.

1. The short version

Your journal entries, moods, tags, photos and audio recordings never leave your phone. There is no account to create, no email address to give, and no server that receives your writing. The app contains no analytics software, no crash-reporting service, no advertising and no third-party tracking. The only thing the app ever sends out is a purchase token, used to confirm a Pulse Pro buy (section 3).

2. What stays on your device

Everything you create is written to your device's private storage:

  • Journal entries (text, titles, tags, pinned and favourite flags)
  • Mood records and your mood history
  • Photos you attach to entries
  • Audio recordings you make for an entry
  • Your seed garden, achievements, streaks, challenges and small habits
  • Letters to your future self
  • Tags, search preferences and app settings

All of this is stored encrypted: the journal database with AES-256, and photos and audio with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is generated on your device and held in the Android Keystore. Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Pulse's data.

No journal content, mood data, photo, audio recording, name or e-mail address is ever included in these requests. Pulse contains no advertising, and no advertising identifier is used — the app even removes the advertising-ID permission that some libraries request on its behalf.

In-app purchases

Payment is handled by Google Play: card details are never seen by this app and never stored on your device. To confirm that a purchase is real and not refunded, Pulse sends Google Play's purchase token, the product ID, and a random identifier that the app generated on your phone to RevenueCat (api.revenuecat.com) over HTTPS. RevenueCat checks that token with Google and answers only whether the purchase is active. This identifier is random, contains nothing about you, and is never joined to your journal data. No other app data is sent, and RevenueCat is used for this verification only — it is not given your writing, your moods, or your identity. The verified result is also stored in the phone's protected key storage so Pulse Pro keeps working after a restart. If you delete the app, the random identifier goes with it; you can ask us to have a stored purchase record removed as well (section 9).

Dictation

Dictation is handed to the speech recogniser already installed on your phone, the same one your keyboard uses. Pulse does not run its own speech service: it asks the phone to listen, receives the words back, and stores them in your entry on your device. Whether that recogniser works offline or sends audio to its own provider is decided by your phone and its settings, not by Pulse. Turning off the microphone permission in your Android settings disables dictation completely.

4. What we do NOT collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any contact details
  • Any account or login information — the app has no sign-in
  • Your location or GPS coordinates
  • Your contacts, call log, or SMS messages
  • Your browsing history or installed apps
  • Usage analytics, advertising identifiers or profiling data
  • Your journal text, mood records, photos, or audio

5. On-device text analysis

Pulse offers features that look at your writing and moods — for example mood patterns, sentiment scores, summaries and tag suggestions. This runs entirely on your device using built-in rules. It is not artificial intelligence hosted in the cloud, and your text is not transmitted to any external service to be analysed.

7. Your control over your data

  • Delete everything. Settings offers a control that erases all journal content, mood history, photos, audio and settings from your device in a single action.
  • Encrypted backup. You can export an encrypted, password-protected backup file and re-import it later. You choose where that file is stored; we never receive it.
  • App lock. You can require a fingerprint, face, or passcode every time the app opens.

8. Children

Pulse is not directed at children under 13. Because the app requires no account and collects no personal information about who you are, it does not knowingly gather data from children. The purchase verification in section 3 carries a random identifier and a purchase token only — nothing that says who the buyer is.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR, CCPA and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Because your journal is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us, you can exercise every one of these rights directly from within the app without contacting us. The one exception is a purchase record: if you bought Pulse Pro, a minimal record (random identifier, purchase token, product) exists at RevenueCat. Mail us the address above and we will have that subscriber record deleted; no journal content is in it.
