Privacy Policy — Shieldra (Phone Security)

Application ID: com.shieldra.app · Android

Effective date: 29 September 2026

Zero permissions · No account · No cloud

Shieldra is a device-first security app for your own phone. This policy explains exactly what Shieldra does and — just as importantly — what it does not do.

1. The short version

Shieldra requests no Android permissions at all and has no account system, no server connection, and no third-party analytics. Everything the app records stays in encrypted storage on your device.

2. Shieldra is designed for your own device

Shieldra protects the phone it is installed on. It is meant to be installed by the phone's own owner to detect tampering, physical movement, and SIM changes. It is not a parental monitoring tool, not a child-tracking app, and not a tool for monitoring another person's phone.

Shieldra cannot see your messages, calls, contacts, photos, browsing, camera, or location, because it does not request permission to access any of them.

3. What Shieldra records

When a guard is triggered, Shieldra may record an event on your device. An event contains only:

  • The type of event (for example, a failed unlock pattern, physical movement, or a SIM change)
  • The date and time it occurred
  • A severity level
  • Any evidence attached to that event, if you have enabled evidence capture
  • Whether a notification was delivered, and to which channel

Event history is kept in a local database, protected by AES-GCM encryption using a key held in the Android Keystore. The key is bound to your device and is deleted if the device is reset.

4. How Shieldra decides to record something

Shieldra uses a two-stage design. A signal on its own never starts a recording. Only a confirmed pattern of signals — for example, several failed unlock attempts within a short window — creates an event. This is deliberate: it means ordinary mistakes do not generate a trail of records.

Shieldra has no server of its own. An alert is delivered only to the destination you configured. The app never sends your event history anywhere, and there is no account to link it to.

Advertising

The free version shows a banner advertisement provided by Google AdMob. The paid lifetime version removes it. For Google's use of the advertising identifier, see Google Ads policies.

In-app purchase

Shieldra offers a one-time lifetime purchase. It is processed by Google Play; the app receives only confirmation that it succeeded. Card details are never seen by the app.

6. What Shieldra does NOT collect

Shieldra does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account details — there is no sign-up
  • Your contacts, call log, or SMS messages
  • Your photos or camera roll, unless you explicitly enable evidence capture for a confirmed event
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • A list of your installed apps
  • Device identifiers such as IMEI or Android ID
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or profiling data
  • Your passcode, passwords, or any authentication secret — the app is designed never to store these

7. Permissions

Shieldra declares zero Android permissions. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, phone state, storage, accessibility, device administration, or the ability to list installed applications.

Any future version that would require a permission will be described in this policy and justified in the app's Play Store listing before release.

8. Backup and transfer

Shieldra is excluded from your device's automatic cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer. Its encrypted event database does not leave the phone through those channels.

9. Data retention and deletion

Event history is stored on your device only. You can delete it from within the app at any time. If you uninstall Shieldra, its local data is removed with it. There is no copy held anywhere else, because none is ever created.

10. Children

Shieldra is intended for adults protecting their own phone and is not directed at children under 13. Because it has no account and collects no personal information, it does not knowingly gather data from children.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR, CCPA and similar laws you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your data. All of Shieldra's data is stored only on your device and never transmitted to us, so you can exercise these rights yourself without contacting us.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new effective date and noted in the app's release notes.
